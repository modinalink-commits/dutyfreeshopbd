Port Delivery Procedure

How delivery to your vessel works at Chattogram, Mongla and Payra, from port call to handover.

Effective 26 November 2026 · Version 1.0

StepHow delivery works
1. Confirm the port callThe buyer or local agent supplies the vessel name, IMO, port, estimated arrival and departure, berth or anchorage, authorised receiver and requested deadline. The Marine Desk confirms the operating details with the agent.
2. Agree the handoverThe order names the exact handover point, such as the authorised berth, launch transfer point or onboard location, and records who arranges each stage and pays the agreed charges. Use Bangladesh time (UTC+6) for the order cut-off and delivery window. Confirm stock, documents, payment or credit, and applicable permissions before dispatch.
3. Prepare and dispatchPack and label the order by vessel and order number. Check quantities, condition and any agreed shelf-life or temperature records. Arrange fulfilment with the shop or port partner authorised for the order. Share the delivery window and contact details with the receiver.
4. Record the handoverThe authorised receiver checks quantities and visible condition at handover where practicable. The delivery note records the items and quantities handed over, shortages or damage, location, local date and time, and the receiver’s name, role and confirmation. Record partial delivery by item.
5. Reconcile the orderShare the delivery note and invoice. Record the quantities accepted and any outstanding items. Agree whether to deliver the balance at a viable slot or cancel it and adjust the invoice or payment under the Returns and Claims Policy.

Handover and risk. Unless the Order Confirmation specifies an agreed alternative, risk of accidental loss or damage passes for the units actually handed over at the agreed point to the buyer or its authorised receiver. Ownership passes according to the agreed sale terms and applicable law. If the parties choose an Incoterms® 2020 rule for a B2B order, record the rule and the exact named place or point in the Order Confirmation and make the handover and risk provisions consistent with it. The delivery note records receipt and visible condition. The buyer retains applicable inspection and defect rights.

Changes, delays and cancellations. If the vessel’s schedule, location, port access or weather changes, the Marine Desk informs the buyer and agent promptly and seeks a revised handover. The buyer approves any revised location, price or additional cost before it is incurred on their account. If completion is impracticable, agree to reschedule or cancel the unfulfilled portion and reconcile any advance, credit and eligible charges in accordance with the order and applicable law. Any applicable mandatory notification and refund deadlines still apply.
