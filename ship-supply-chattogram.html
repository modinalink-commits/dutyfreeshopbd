Ship Supply at Chattogram Port

Duty-free stores, provisions and crew supplies for vessels calling at Chattogram, Bangladesh. Quoted in USD and delivered on board.

About the port

Chattogram, also written Chittagong, is the principal seaport of Bangladesh on the Karnaphuli River. Vessels are served both alongside at the jetties and terminals and at the outer anchorage.

How to order

Register your vessel by IMO number and tell us your ETA and berth or anchorage. Send your requisition from the catalog or as an Excel or PDF list. Our marine desk replies with a quotation in USD, and delivery is arranged for your port stay.
