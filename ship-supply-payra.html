Ship Supply at Payra Port

Duty-free stores, provisions and crew supplies for vessels calling at Payra, Bangladesh. Quoted in USD and delivered on board.

About the port

Payra is the newest seaport of Bangladesh, in Patuakhali district on the southern coast. As traffic to the port grows we are preparing supply for vessels calling at its terminal and anchorage.

What we supply

  • General ship stores: provisions, beverages, confectionery and toiletries
  • Bonded stores for verified accounts, released from the port's licensed duty-free shop
  • Personal orders for crew members, within the vessel's allowance

How delivery works at Payra

  • Delivery at terminal and anchorage
  • Requisition and quotation before arrival
  • Opening after Chattogram
  • Signed delivery note and USD invoice for every supply

How to order

Register your vessel by IMO number and tell us your ETA and berth or anchorage. Send your requisition from the catalog or as an Excel or PDF list. Our marine desk replies with a quotation in USD, and delivery is arranged for your port stay.
